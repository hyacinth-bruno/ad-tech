How to demonstrate the set

  1. Open the campaign console and search for [Demo to isolate these 40 examples. Status tabs demonstrate each lifecycle state; click a status badge to change it.
  2. Use a shopping-context link below. Algolia ranks overlapping eligible campaigns, so the displayed campaign may be another relevant example. A link does not force a specific ad.
  3. Click Discover more on a served ad to open its local product page. Add it to the bag and complete demo checkout if you want a demonstration order. No payment is collected.
  4. For repeatable cap checks, use the console’s live preview with a fixed user ID and matching category/placement. Examples 08–12 cover caps of 1, 3, 5 and 50. Counters reset at UTC midnight; a fresh user ID starts with fresh counters.
  5. For narrow targeting, select All Refrigerators explicitly. For punctuation handling, select Coffee, Tea & Espresso. Categories match exact stored values.

Verified behavior and current limits

  • All 40 generated campaign states verified; 32 active campaigns verified in Algolia; all eight inactive generated campaigns verified as ineligible by status. The 37 campaigns that existed before this addition were preserved.
  • All 40 advertised SKUs resolve to priced catalog products. Search, category and product-page ad requests each returned a generated campaign.
  • Verification checked all three placements, confirmed Demo 08 serves once and then returns no ad for the same test user, and confirmed an unmatched category returns no ad. These checks and browser navigation generated normal delivery/click events. No purchase orders were created.
  • Budgets are illustrative EUR settings in the campaign console. Product prices are historical USD sample prices. No actual ad spend is charged.
  • Budgets and dates are stored but are not enforced by serving. Future-date examples remain draft; past-date examples are ended. These are configuration examples, not proof of scheduling or pacing.
  • Keyword matching uses Algolia relevance. Brand is searchable content, not a dedicated targeting constraint. Selection has no bidding auction or guaranteed ordering.
  • Campaign impressions count served responses, not verified views. Search purchase analytics and ad click counts exist separately; purchase-to-campaign attribution and ROAS are not implemented.
  • To demonstrate no-fill, request a deliberately unmatched category in live preview, or a query with no matching campaign. To demonstrate invalid input, use form validation; invalid campaigns were not saved.
  • Personalization, re-ranking, NeuralSearch, billing, Google Ad Manager integration and attribution are outside this generated campaign set and cannot be activated by creating campaign records.
